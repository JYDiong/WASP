Rainfall Forecast Viewer (3-Hourly Accumulation)

This page presents the 3-hourly accumulated rainfall forecast from the Global Forecast System (GFS) over Malaysia for the next 5 days.

Rainfall is shown in millimeters (mm), with values indicating the total precipitation expected within each 3-hour period. Use the slider or ← / → arrow keys to explore how rainfall evolves over time and identify potential periods of heavy precipitation.

These forecasts are helpful for anticipating localized flooding, storm development, and general wet weather impacts.

Tip: Use ← and → arrow keys to step 3 hours
